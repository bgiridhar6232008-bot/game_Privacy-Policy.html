Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

Effective Date: October 2, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 2, 2026
Application Name: Arrow Escape (Package ID: com.aistudio.arrowescape.qpzvxr)

Thank you for playing Arrow Escape ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you download, install, and play the Arrow Escape mobile application available on Google Play.

Summary: Arrow Escape does not require you to create an account or submit personal contact details. All puzzle progress across the 100 levels is saved locally on your device. The App displays advertisements via Google AdMob (App Open Ads and Rewarded Ads), which may collect device identifiers and diagnostic data as described below.

1. Information We Collect and Store Locally

Local Game Progress: Arrow Escape stores your gameplay progress—including unlocked levels (Levels 1–100), stars earned, remaining hints, and audio/haptic/theme preferences—locally on your device using an on-device SQLite (Room) database. This data is never transmitted to our own servers.

You can permanently delete all locally stored game progress at any time directly inside the App by opening Settings (Gear Icon) → Reset All Progress, or by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements within the App, including App Open Ads when the application launches and optional Rewarded Ads when you choose to refill hearts, earn hints, or add bonus level time.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable and device-level information to provide, measure, and fraud-check advertisements, including:

  • Device and Advertising Identifiers: Your device's Google Advertising ID (AAID), which you can reset or opt out of in your Android Settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Coarse Location / IP Address: Used to estimate general geographic region for relevant ad delivery and regulatory compliance.
  • App Interaction & Ad Performance Data: Information about ad impressions, ad clicks, and rewarded ad completions.
  • Device Diagnostics: Device make, model, operating system version, screen dimensions, language, and crash/performance logs.

For complete details on how Google collects and processes data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob & AdSense Partner Policies

3. Permissions Used by the App

Arrow Escape requests only the minimum Android permissions required for gameplay and advertising:

  • android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display App Open Ads and Rewarded Ads.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used to provide tactile haptic feedback when tapping arrows or encountering obstacles (can be toggled off anytime in the in-app Settings menu).

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • On-Device Game Data: Retained locally on your device for as long as the App remains installed. You can erase it immediately via the in-app Reset All Progress button or by uninstalling the App.
  • Advertising Data: Managed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. You may reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device's system settings.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the email address below so we can take appropriate action.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California), you have rights regarding your data, including the right to opt out of personalized advertising. You can exercise these rights at any time by:

  • Navigating to Settings → Google → All Services → Ads on your Android device and selecting Delete advertising ID or opting out of Ads Personalization.
  • Uninstalling the App or resetting in-app data via the Settings menu.

7. Security

All network communications made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK use industry-standard encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Local gameplay data is sandboxed within Android's private application storage directory.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
